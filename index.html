03 / Studio

Policy, patterns and standards

What a service says to people, and the rules it has to meet while saying it.

  • government-communication

    The front door. Drafts, adapts or reviews a message for your service, or plans the messages a case journey needs.

    Copilot · soonClaude skill: government-communication
  • case-communication-patterns

    One pattern for each moment in a case, like "we've received it" or "we couldn't reach you".

    Copilot · soonClaude skill: case-communication-patterns
  • govuk-content

    Drafts, rewrites and reviews customer content against GOV.UK style, with a plain English check.

    Copilot · soonClaude skill: govuk-content
  • privacy-aware-communications

    Checks a message in each channel against UK GDPR and ICO guidance, and can draft the communications part of a DPIA for your DPO to review.

    Copilot · soonClaude skill: privacy-aware-communications

Using the skills in Claude

Each zip holds one skill folder. Unzip it into your project's skills folder, open the project and ask for what you need. The right skill picks itself up.

unzip govuk-content.zip -d .claude/skills
